## What's new in 1.1.0

**Recording**
- **Mute button.** Mute your mic mid-recording for off-the-record moments. The mic icon also shows your live input level while recording.
- **Better "You" vs. other speakers.** Fixed cases where the other person's voice, picked up by your mic from your speakers, was labeled as you.
- **Setup tip for system audio.** If a recording starts without system audio, Recap now explains how to set up BlackHole and a Multi-Output Device. It shows once with Dismiss, then with Dismiss and Ignore.

**Transcript and notes**
- **Merge speaker labels.** If one person was split into two speakers, merge them in the transcript, undo if needed, then use Regenerate notes to rebuild the summary.
- **Notes detail setting.** A Quick / Balanced / Detailed slider in Settings > Recording controls how much the notes include.
- **Missing-topic check.** After a Balanced or Detailed summary, Recap flags substantive topics the notes skipped and offers to add them. It is an extra check, so it can occasionally flag something minor or miss something.

**App**
- **Appearance setting.** Light, Dark or System, in Settings > General.
- **Update notice.** When a newer release exists, a small notice offers Download, Later or Skip. Turn it off under Settings > General.
- Several small polish fixes to spacing and alignment.
